Build an entry for make.fun's "The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse" bounty
(slug u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36).

The bounty: write one line, under 200 characters, that someone could send to
call in sick — the funniest excuse that lands in one breath, and nothing else.
Total prize $2 in USDC, top 3 paid: 1st $1.00, 2nd $0.50, 3rd $0.50. The goal
is to land inside the paid top 3, not just to produce a valid line.

Entries close 2026-11-27T17:10:00Z (28 Nov 00:10 WIB); results are due
19 Dec 2026. Blind round 1 runs until 25 Nov 17:11:28Z.

How it is judged: a judge model compares the lines two at a time. It weighs,
most important first: whether the excuse is half believable and gets a laugh;
whether the idea is fresh rather than a worn fever, dog or dead-grandmother
excuse; whether the twist or funniest word arrives near the end; whether it
sounds like a real person typing a message; and whether every word is doing
work. Length for its own sake does not count. There is no tie: the judge must
pick one line in every pair, and a decisive win counts far more than a close
one, so the line has to be clearly funnier than the average entry, not just
marginally better.

What the entry is: a Node 22 program, main.ts at the repository root, that
writes exactly one line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (falling back to
/output). The file must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters, a single line,
not only whitespace — anything else is never judged and takes no prize.

The line this entry submits: "Can't come in today — my phone autocorrected
'sick day' to 'I quit' in yesterday's text, so HR says I'm already gone."
(117 characters.) It works because autocorrect mishaps are something every
boss has lived through, the idea is fresh, and the punchline — "I'm already
gone" — arrives on the last words, in the voice of a real text message.
